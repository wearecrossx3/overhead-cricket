**App name:** Overhead Cricket

**Full description:**
Overhead Cricket is fast, satisfying T20 cricket in a bold illustrated style.

🏏 ONE-TAP BATTING — Tap when the ring turns green. Your timing aims the shot: early to leg, late to off, perfect goes straight. Swipe up to launch it into the stands, swipe down to defend. Hit 3 perfect shots in a row to go ON FIRE.
🎥 3 CAMERA ANGLES — Classic Stick-style, Isometric 3D or Top view.
⚾ REAL CRICKET LAWS — Wides, no-balls & free hits, byes, leg-byes, LBW, stumpings, run-outs, hit wicket and repeated Super Overs.
🎯 BOWL YOUR WAY — Pace or spin, yorkers, bouncers, slower balls; set line & length and nail the release meter.
💰 IPL-STYLE AUCTION — Build your franchise with a coin purse: marquee sets, bid wars, overseas limits and an accelerated round.
🏆 FRANCHISE LEAGUE — Round robin, points table with NRR, Qualifiers, Eliminator and the Final.
⭐ CHALLENGES, NETS & SHOP — 12 pressure scenarios with stars, practice nets, bats that hit harder, daily rewards and trophies.
🌙 Day & night matches with floodlights, packed stands and celebrations.

No ads. No sign-in. Works offline.
