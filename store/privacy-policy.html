Overhead Cricket — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Overhead Cricket is made by Frontpage Studios. The game does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • Your progress (coins, stats, league, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The game does not use analytics, advertising IDs, location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • The only permission used is vibration, for haptic feedback.

Uninstalling the app deletes all saved data. Questions: thefrontpagestudio@gmail.com
